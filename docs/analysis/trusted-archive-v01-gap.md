## Shared behavior and release evidence

An answer capsule is the compact prose summary accompanied by a date and source. `web/lib/geo-capsules.ts` chooses the **first valid** date candidate, not the newest date or the oldest common cutoff. `resolveDataAsOfFromMeta` prefers `generated_at`, then `backfilled_at`, then `folded_through.month`, before entity-specific fallback dates. Thus a valid global generation date can mask older entity history. This applies in both locales; shared translated capsule/FAQ text lives in `web/app/_localized/seo-copy.ts` and `web/app/_localized/detail-copy.ts`, with dictionaries in `web/lib/i18n/dictionaries/en.ts` and `ja.ts`.

The claimed footer freshness is absent on this Astro implementation: `astro/src/components/Footer.astro` conditionally renders data-through only if `asOf` is supplied, but `astro/src/layouts/Layout.astro` never supplies it. All inspected successful English/Japanese pages end with source/license attribution and no data-through date. Footer attribution alone is not a page-specific citation recipe.

No public refresh-history index was found in `astro/src/pages/`, inspected page links, or About's source/export links. `/releases` and `/ja/releases` returned 404. There **are public per-run files**, so release evidence is partial rather than wholly absent:

- [views/latest.json](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/views/latest.json) returned `version` and `run_id` equal to `refresh-2026-10-25T11-36-27-410Z`, `published_at=2026-10-25T13:01:02.673Z`, `prev_version=refresh-2026-10-21T21-01-25-223Z`, and `schema_ver=1`. It has no repository or skip counts and is a moving pointer, not a list of past refreshes.
- The [current publish record](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/ops/workflows/refresh-2026-10-25T11-36-27-410Z/publish-intent.json) retains those version/date/previous-version fields. The [previous publish record](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/ops/workflows/refresh-2026-10-21T21-01-25-223Z/publish-intent.json) is also readable: published October 21 at `22:08:08.586Z`, with `prev_version=null`. These records permit following this observed two-release chain, but are not linked from reader pages or assembled into a history file.
- The [current run manifest](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/ops/workflows/refresh-2026-10-25T11-36-27-410Z/manifest.json) exposes `run_id`, `started_at`, `status=published`, step names, and `published_version`. [latest-success.json](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/ops/workflows/latest-success.json) repeats the successful version/publication time, not a history or count summary. Persistence comes from `web/lib/workflows/checkpoint.ts`, `web/lib/workflows/publication-core.ts`, and `web/lib/workflows/publication-deps.ts`; Astro uses the shared workflow through `astro/src/packages/p7/operations.ts`. No workflow was executed for this audit.
- The [current version's metadata](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/views/refresh-2026-10-25T11-36-27-410Z/meta.json) exposes `active_repo_count=5687`, `historical_repo_count=0`, `generated_at=2026-10-25T12:42:09.205Z`, `seam_date=2026-10-21`, and folded-through month/week `2026-09` / `2026-W40`. The [previous metadata](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/views/refresh-2026-10-21T21-01-25-223Z/meta.json) reports 5,669 active and zero historical repositories. These generation times/counts belong to their versions, not to export files.
- Per-bucket metadata progress is publicly readable at `ops/workflows/<run_id>/metadata-0.json` through `metadata-31.json` ([first current bucket](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/ops/workflows/refresh-2026-10-25T11-36-27-410Z/metadata-0.json)). All 32 current buckets were read: summed `fetched` entries = 5,687, `skipped` array lengths = 0, pending pages = 0. This is a derived skip count, not a scalar field in latest.json. `web/lib/workflows/steps/metadata.ts` returns `skipped_count` and `skipped` (including reason and retained/missing value status), and persists `skipped` per bucket. `web/lib/workflows/runtime/graph.ts` aggregates repositories/history/fetched counts but does not carry a summed skip count into the publication pointer. An absent progress file must not be interpreted as zero skips.
- [live/latest.json](https://data-pre.gitstarclub.com/live/latest.json) separately identifies the weekly activity generation `weekly-2026-10-25T04-00-34-331Z`, published at `2026-10-25T04:06:10.366Z`, with `previous_generation=daily-2026-10-24T03-01-00-596Z`. Activity and managed-refresh publication times differ on the same calendar day. `web/lib/data/snapshot.ts` and `astro/src/lib/published-readers.ts` explain the separate reader inputs; no page-level release link explains that distinction.
- The [latest export manifest](https://pre.gitstarclub.com/data/exports/v1/latest/manifest.json) and [dated manifest](https://pre.gitstarclub.com/data/exports/v1/2026-09-06/manifest.json) identify a September 6 export even though About says October 25. The Astro worker rewrites latest aliases to the packaged export date. Dated export directories are existing citation evidence, but do not list every managed refresh or connect exports to its version. `web/lib/workflows/steps/gc.ts` keeps a bounded number of data versions (four newest plus protected versions), so a versioned data URL alone does not establish indefinite retention.

## Published fields available but not surfaced where needed

- **Release identity and lineage:** `version`, `run_id`, `published_at`, `prev_version`, `schema_ver` in the pointer and per-run publish records; none are displayed or linked by the inspected reader pages. Run `status`, `started_at`, and `published_version` could supply publication evidence alongside them.
- **Set counts:** version metadata `active_repo_count` and `historical_repo_count` are absent from About's snapshot and most page-level scope statements. Category maturity happens to show the active-set count, but does not replace a consistent archive-wide coverage statement.
- **Layer-specific dates and rules:** metadata `seam_date` / `folded_through.week`, individual rank/registry `generated_at`, category `rules_version` (observed `2026-06-07.2`), and live-generation identity/previous generation are not collected into an explicit per-layer fact block. About already shows folded-through month; the gap is the remaining layer context, not absence of all dates.
- **Skip evidence:** public per-bucket `skipped` arrays and workflow result `skipped_count` can feed a release summary, but require aggregation and completeness checks. No public consolidated skip total is present in the pointer/run manifest, and no reader page displays one.
- **Export metadata at the download entry point:** `export_date`, `data_as_of`, `limits`, `files[].rows`, `source_views`, and `dated_urls` already exist in downloadable metadata. Both About pages omit the actual export date/counts beside download links. The files have no producing refresh identifier to surface yet.
