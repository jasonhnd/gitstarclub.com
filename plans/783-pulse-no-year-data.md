# Pulse empty yearly state

## Goal
Show a localized “No yearly data yet” state in Pulse's YEAR column and leader card when no annual ranking is published.

## Scope
Update the Astro Pulse presentation and all seven locale dictionaries. Add coverage for empty and populated annual data. Capture before/after Pulse screenshots at 1440px and 390px for English and Japanese.

## Out of scope
Changing published data, pipeline behavior, other pages, deployment, or Cloudflare configuration.

## Acceptance
The empty state appears in both annual Pulse placements for all locales, populated annual data still renders normally, and requested screenshots are available for review.
